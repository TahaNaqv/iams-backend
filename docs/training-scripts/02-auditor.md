# Script 02 — Auditor walkthrough

**Audience:** auditors · **Length:** ~15 min · **Pre-req:** script 01

End-to-end audit cycle: open an assigned engagement, walk a checklist, attach evidence, raise a finding, open a CAP, write a working paper, route for review, sign off.

---

| Time | Narration | On-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | "This walkthrough follows a single audit from kick-off to sign-off. By the end you'll know how IAMS handles checklists, evidence, findings, CAPs, working papers, and the sign-off workflow." | Title card "Auditor walkthrough". |
| 0:30–1:00 | "Open the dashboard. *My audits* lists your assignments. Click the Q1 Treasury audit." | Dashboard → My audits panel → click row. |
| 1:00–1:45 | "The audit detail page has six tabs: Overview, Checklist, Evidence, Findings, Working Papers, Timeline. Overview shows scope, objectives, status, and the lead auditor. The checklist drives your fieldwork — each item has owner, due date, and a tick-box. Items roll up into the audit completion percentage." | Audit page → tour each tab quickly. Pause on Checklist. |
| 1:45–2:30 | "Tick off a checklist item by toggling its status to *Complete*. The completion percent updates immediately. If an item is blocked, switch its status to *On Hold* — the audit manager sees a flag on the dashboard." | Toggle a checklist item; show the audit progress bar move. |
| 2:30–3:30 | "Click *Evidence* to attach a control test result. Drag the PDF onto the drop zone — the system scans it for malware before storing. Files larger than 100MB are rejected; PDFs and Office docs are fine." | Drag-and-drop a PDF; show AV scanning indicator → green checkmark. |
| 3:30–4:30 | "Time to raise a finding. Click *Findings → Add Finding*. Give it a title, pick severity, describe the issue, root cause, and recommendation. Set an owner and a due date. Severity drives the urgency of the notification — Critical pages the owner immediately, Medium just goes to their inbox." | Open finding modal; fill in fields; submit. The finding appears in the list with a Critical severity pill. |
| 4:30–5:15 | "Once a finding is filed, you open a Corrective Action Plan — a CAP — against it. Click *Add CAP*, give it a title, owner, due date, priority. The CAP appears under the finding. When the auditee finishes their work and you approve the closure, the CAP transitions to Closed automatically." | Open CAP modal; submit. CAP row appears nested under the finding. |
| 5:15–6:30 | "Working papers document your testing. Open the Working Papers tab → *New*. Use the rich-text editor for the methodology, evidence summary, and conclusion. Cross-reference another paper with double-square-brackets and the title — it auto-links. Save the draft." | Compose a working paper. Type `[[`. Show the autocomplete dropdown. |
| 6:30–7:30 | "When fieldwork is complete, sign as auditor — that's the *Sign as Auditor* button. Now another team member needs to sign as reviewer. The system enforces separation of duties: you can't review your own paper. Once both signatures are in, the paper is locked." | Click Sign. Show the signature line. Switch to a different user; click Sign as Reviewer. Paper status flips to Locked. |
| 7:30–8:15 | "Need to revise a signed paper? Click *Create New Version*. The old version becomes immutable and stays in history; the new version inherits the cross-references and goes back into draft. The audit trail captures every transition." | Click Create New Version. Show the version chain in the sidebar. |
| 8:15–9:15 | "Findings can be challenged. If the auditee disagrees, they hit *Challenge* on the finding — that opens a discussion thread visible to both of you. The CAP timeline isn't paused; the challenge runs in parallel. Resolve the challenge with a comment when you're done." | Open a finding → Challenge tab → show a sample thread. |
| 9:15–10:30 | "Risk assessment lives under *Risk Register*. The Risk Workbook lets you score auditable entities against the configured factors — likelihood, impact, control strength, and whatever else your model defines. Click *Record Score*, enter the values, submit. The composite is normalized to 0–100. Entities above the threshold are flagged High Risk automatically." | Open Risk Workbook → record a score → show the new composite. |
| 10:30–11:30 | "When you want a PDF report for your engagement, go to *Reports → Generate*. Pick *Audit Summary*, point it at this audit, hit Generate. The job runs async; you'll get a notification when the PDF is ready with a download link. Excel exports work the same way for tabular data." | Generate Audit Summary; show the in-progress toast; click the notification when it appears; click Download. |
| 11:30–12:30 | "Notifications you'll see day-to-day: CAP due in three days, CAP overdue, finding raised on your audit, approval requested. The weekly digest summarizes everything that landed since last Monday — it arrives Monday at 8 AM local." | Open the bell; scroll through sample notifications. |
| 12:30–13:30 | "Stuck on an audit? Use the *Time Entry* tab to log hours against the engagement — your manager sees the burndown on their dashboard. If you're going over budget, raise it early — IAMS shows the budget vs. actual right on the audit overview." | Open Time Entry; log 3 hours. Show the budget burndown bar. |
| 13:30–14:30 | "Final tips. The audit log under *Settings → Audit Log* records everything you've ever done — useful for your own QAIP review. The Help icon in the topbar pulls up page-specific help. Press question-mark anywhere in the app for keyboard shortcuts." | Open the audit log scoped to the current user. Trigger the keyboard shortcuts modal. |
| 14:30–15:00 | "You're set. Next in the playlist: Audit Manager workflow — approvals, annual plan generation, and QAIP dashboards." | Outro card. |
