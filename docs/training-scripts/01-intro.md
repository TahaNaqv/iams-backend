# Script 01 — Introduction

**Audience:** everyone · **Length:** ~5 min · **Pre-req:** none

A high-level tour so a first-time user understands the landing experience and finds their feet. Doesn't go deep on any role — that's what the role-specific scripts are for.

---

| Time | Narration | On-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | "Welcome to IAMS, your Internal Audit Management System. This short tour will get you signed in, oriented, and ready to do work." | Title card with the IAMS logo. |
| 0:20–0:45 | "Most users sign in through your corporate single-sign-on — click the blue *Sign in with corporate account* button. If you're a service account or breaking-glass for an emergency, use the username and password form." | Login page. Highlight the SSO button, then the password fields. |
| 0:45–1:15 | "If your role requires multi-factor authentication, the first time you sign in IAMS prompts you to enroll an authenticator app. Scan the QR with Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy, then confirm the six-digit code. Don't forget to **save your backup codes** — they're shown once and each works only once." | MFA enrollment page; QR code + backup-code grid. Overlay text: "Save these — they're shown only once." |
| 1:15–1:45 | "You land on a dashboard tailored to your role. Executives see the four headline KPIs, the year-over-year trend chart, and the risk heat map by department. Auditors see their assigned audits, their open findings, and what's coming up." | Executive dashboard → fade to Auditor dashboard. Cursor traces each panel. |
| 1:45–2:15 | "Every KPI tile and every chart segment is clickable. Click *Pending CAPs* to drill into the underlying list, filtered to just those items." | Click "Pending CAPs" → filtered list animates in. |
| 2:15–2:45 | "The left nav groups everything by function — Audit Planning, Audit Execution, Findings & CAPs, Risk Register, Compliance, Reports, Settings. The topbar is for notifications, your profile, and language." | Pan across the sidebar; hover each section. |
| 2:45–3:15 | "Speaking of language — IAMS ships in English, Arabic, and French. Pick yours from the User menu; the Arabic layout mirrors right-to-left automatically." | Open language switcher. Select Arabic — show the layout flip. Switch back to English. |
| 3:15–3:45 | "The bell in the top right is your inbox. Audit assignments, CAP deadlines, approval requests, and weekly digests all land here. The badge count is live — refreshes every minute." | Click the bell. Show a few sample notifications. Read one aloud. |
| 3:45–4:15 | "Need to change your password, set up MFA, or regenerate your backup codes? Settings → Account → Security has all of that. Your password must be at least 12 characters and can't match your last five." | Navigate to Settings → Security. Highlight the policy text. |
| 4:15–4:45 | "Help is always one click away. The question-mark icon in the topbar pulls up context-aware help for the page you're on. The user guide and admin guide are linked from there too." | Click the help icon → side panel opens. |
| 4:45–5:00 | "That's the orientation. Pick the role-specific video from the playlist for a deeper walkthrough — Auditor, Audit Manager, Auditee, or IT Admin. Happy auditing." | Outro card with the four role-video thumbnails. |
