# Script 04 — Auditee walkthrough

**Audience:** auditees (control owners, department heads) · **Length:** ~5 min · **Pre-req:** script 01

What an auditee actually does in IAMS: respond to a CAP, attach evidence, fill in a CSA questionnaire, challenge a finding.

---

| Time | Narration | On-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | "This short walkthrough is for auditees — anyone who owns a control or remediation. If you're not raising findings yourself but responding to them, this is the video for you." | Title card. |
| 0:25–1:00 | "When a CAP is assigned to you, you'll see two things: an email in your inbox, and a notification on the IAMS bell. Click the bell notification to jump straight into the CAP." | Email arrives. Switch to IAMS. Click bell → CAP opens. |
| 1:00–1:45 | "Read the recommendation and the auditor's expected outcome. The right rail shows the parent finding, the severity, and the due date. The audit trail at the bottom shows everyone who's touched this CAP since it was opened." | Walk through the CAP detail page. |
| 1:45–2:45 | "Attach your remediation evidence — screenshot, updated policy doc, test result. Drag the file onto the Evidence tab; the system scans it for malware before storing. Use the comment field to explain what the evidence shows." | Drag a PDF onto Evidence. AV scan icon → green check. Add a comment. |
| 2:45–3:30 | "Update *Progress* as you work — 25%, 50%, all the way to 100%. When you hit 100%, the auditor automatically gets a 'ready for closure' notification. They review your evidence and approve or reject closure." | Move the slider from 30% to 100%. Show the notification that fires. |
| 3:30–4:15 | "Disagree with the finding? Click *Challenge*. That starts a discussion thread with the auditor without pausing the CAP timeline. You're not denying the issue — you're documenting your disagreement so the audit log captures it. Resolve the challenge with a comment once you've talked it through." | Click Challenge. Type a position. Submit. |
| 4:15–4:45 | "You may also receive Control Self-Assessment questionnaires. They're in your inbox and on your dashboard. Open one, answer each question — single choice, rating 1 to 5, or text — and submit. Weak controls auto-create a follow-up alert to the audit manager." | Open a CSA questionnaire. Answer 3 questions. Submit. |
| 4:45–5:00 | "That's the auditee flow. Quick to learn, quick to use — the goal is to keep your remediation work moving without making you fight the tool." | Outro card. |
